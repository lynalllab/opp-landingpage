Co-production team

People with lived experience

People with lived experience of mental illness sit in the core team, shaping the questions OPP asks, how data is governed, and how findings are shared.

We aim to bring together 12–16 people with lived experience across our workshop series, including a core group of 3–4 who will stay involved throughout.
