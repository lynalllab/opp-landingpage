Help us shape the future of mental health research and inform drug development

We are inviting industry experts to highlight datasets currently missing or inaccessible, including e.g. longitudinal, EHR-linked outcome data across cohorts and cohort-level prevalence and biomarker distribution data.

OPP will offer a single federated query layer across TREs and access to ancestrally and geographically diverse cohorts but we could offer much more based on what industry tells us.

By incorporating industry expertise, we aim to build the OPP and implement a tiered access model that sustains the resource.

Work with us

Is your company interested in data access, precision mental health, biomarkers, functional-genomics based pipeline de-risking, or side effect prediction? Let us know what your organisation needs.
